Chicken Milanese Hero With Broccoli Rabe and Provolone

Odin Recipes

Description

Whenever you make chicken cutlets, making extras pays off for a great second-day sandwich. This classic hero stacks crispy breaded chicken Milanese with sharp aged provolone and garlicky sautéed broccoli rabe finished with a squeeze of fresh lemon. It works equally well with pork or veal cutlets, too.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts, butterflied and pounded
  • Kosher salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs (beaten)
  • 1 and half; cups unseasoned Italian-style breadcrumbs
  • 6 tbsp light extra-virgin olive oil (plus extra for sautéing and drizzling)
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 big bunch of broccoli rabe, tough ends trimmed and discarded
  • 4 large slices sharp, aged provolone (such as Auricchio Stravecchio)
  • 4 Italian-style submarine rolls (or short baguettes)
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes

Let's Cook!

  1. Preheat the oven to warm (200F). Bring a large, covered pot of heavily salted water to a rolling boil.
  2. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper, then dredge in flour, dip in egg, and coat completely in breadcrumbs.
  3. Sauté the chicken in a large skillet over medium-high heat with plenty of olive oil until golden brown outside and moist inside, then hold in the warmed oven.
  4. Blanch the broccoli rabe in the boiling water for 3 minutes, then drain.
  5. Meanwhile, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a second pan over medium heat with the minced garlic and red pepper flakes, stirring frequently until just beginning to brown.
  6. Add the broccoli rabe and sauté, turning once or twice until tender (add a few tablespoons of water if needed). Finish with at least 2 tablespoons of olive oil, a squeeze of lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.
  7. Turn the oven to broil. Slice the rolls lengthwise without cutting all the way through so they open like a book, drizzle the inside with olive oil, and lightly toast under the broiler.

Plating the Sandwich

Layer the warm chicken cutlets and sautéed broccoli rabe onto one side of the toasted roll and place the provolone slices on the other. Return to the broiler until the cheese just starts to melt, then close the sandwich, cut it in half, and serve immediately.
