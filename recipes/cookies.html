Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Odin Recipes

Description

These classic chocolate chip cookies bake up with crisp, golden edges and soft, chewy centers loaded with melted chocolate. Using a mix of brown and white sugar gives them a rich caramel flavor while keeping the middle tender. On a personal touch, I like to finish them with a light pinch of flaky sea salt right when they come out of the oven.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4; cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened to room temperature)
  • 3/4; cup packed light brown sugar
  • 3/4; cup granulated white sugar
  • 2 large eggs (at room temperature)
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips (or chopped chocolate)
  • (optional) Flaky sea salt for topping

Let's Cook!

  1. Preheat the oven to 375F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl, then set aside.
  3. Beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and white sugar in a large bowl until smooth and creamy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  5. Gradually mix the dry flour mixture into the wet ingredients just until no white streaks remain, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart.
  7. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until the edges are golden brown while the centers still look slightly soft.

Plating the Cookies

Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes so they set, then transfer them to a wire rack. Stack a few warm cookies on a small plate, sprinkle lightly with flaky sea salt if using, and serve alongside a cold glass of milk.
