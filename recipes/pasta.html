Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Odin Recipes

Description

Spaghetti aglio e olio (garlic and oil) is a very simple Italian dish to make. With just a few ingredients you can make a very fast and delicious meal. On a personal touch, I usually prefer to add hot pepper seeds instead of black pepper.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti (Preferably fresh)
  • olive oil
  • fresh parsley
  • fresh garlic
  • crushed red pepper
  • fresh parmesan cheese
  • salt to taste
  • (optional) cherry tomatoes for a little color

Let's Cook!

  1. Fill a large pot with cold water, add a generous pinch of salt, and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. While the water heats, peel and slice the garlic into medium-thin slices (not paper-thin so they don't burn).
  3. Once the water boils, add the spaghetti without breaking it; let the submerged half soften, then gently push the rest under. Stir occasionally so it doesn't stick, and cook until just shy of al dente (about 7–8 minutes).
  4. Meanwhile, warm a generous amount of olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and cook gently until fragrant and pale golden, taking care not to brown it. (Optional: add a pinch of red pepper flakes during the last 30 seconds).
  5. Reserve about ½ cup of the starchy pasta water, then drain the spaghetti and transfer it directly into the pan with the garlic oil.
  6. Toss vigorously over low heat, adding splashes of the reserved pasta water until the oil and water emulsify into a light, glossy sauce that coats the noodles.
  7. Remove from heat, toss in freshly chopped flat-leaf parsley, and serve immediately.

Plating the Spaghetti

Twirl a portion of spaghetti into a neat pile in the center of a warm bowl using a fork and a large spoon. Spoon some of the leftover garlic oil and golden garlic slices over the top, then finish with a sprinkle of fresh parsley, a pinch of red pepper flakes, and a light drizzle of olive oil.
